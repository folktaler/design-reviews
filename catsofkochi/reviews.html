State B. Turning one down. The reason is not a category — it is the sentence the writer receives, and that makes it design, not admin.

Why are you turning this down?

Pick one. They get the sentence underneath it.

  • It reads like an advert "Thanks for writing in. We have not put this one up — we do not publish reviews that promote a business or carry a phone number."
  • It names a member of staff "Thanks for writing. We have left this one off because it names someone who works there. Send it again without the name and we will put it up."
  • We cannot tell which place it is about "Thanks for writing. We could not work out which place this was about, so we have not put it up. Send it again and name the place?"
  • It is about something we cannot check "Thanks for writing. We have not put this one up, because it makes a claim about the place we have no way to look into."
  • It insults somebody "Thanks for writing. We have not put this up. We do not publish reviews that insult people."

Nothing is deleted. A turned-down review stays in the "Turned down" list with the reason, so a decision can be looked at again.
