Cats of Kochi — the Airbnb-faithful system

Labelled catsofkochi-airbnb-design-system-2026-08-09 — a different system from the earlier Guardian-era one (guardian-design-system-v1), stated here so nobody reviews the wrong one by name. 13 pages, one shared faces file per font family, no build step and almost no JavaScript. Every kit shows the current typeface, Figtree.

The Android fallback arm is unverified by rendering. Fallback metrics for Noto Serif and Roboto are derived from @capsizecss's published metrics, not measured on an Android device.

This site renders in standards mode; the Claude Design preview does not. Every kit published there reports document.compatMode: "BackCompat" (quirks) because that host's serve wrapper injects markup ahead of the doctype — not our markup, not fixable there. Where the two differ, trust this one.

Accounts and reviews

  • Visitor reviews The buildable kit — design-system/ui_kits/reviews/. Leaving a review, what the writer sees while it waits and if it is turned down, how a score and a breakdown render once reviews accumulate, and the queue where every submission is accepted or turned down. Every name, star and count on it is invented.
  • Signing in, and the fields The buildable kit — design-system/ui_kits/account/. The header signed out and signed in, sign in, make an account, the form that waits, and every state a field is allowed to be in: default, optional, wrong, cannot-be-used, done.
  • Visitor reviews — the original three screens Superseded by Visitor reviews, which carries the same decisions plus the states this one did not draw. Kept because it is the page the owner reacted to.
