Cats of Kochi — the Airbnb-faithful system

Labelled catsofkochi-airbnb-design-system-2026-08-09 — a different system from the earlier Guardian-era one (guardian-design-system-v1), stated here so nobody reviews the wrong one by name. 13 pages, one shared faces file per font family, no build step and no JavaScript. 12 of the 13 kits now show the current typeface, Figtree. One does not, deliberately — read the gap below before judging its type.

Known gaps — read this before reviewing

  • ⭐ type_before_after still shows Noticia Text, and stays that way on purpose. The owner decided, 9 August 2026: Cats of Kochi is Figtree, all-sans — no second family (the other twelve kits carry that decision as of today; they were rebuilt from the Guardian-era serif this same day). This one kit is different in kind, not just behind: it is a dated 5 August essay arguing for Noticia Text — file-size measurements against eight fonts, a check for the ₹ glyph, a Guardian-line-spacing table — not a live specimen. Repointing its face without rewriting that whole argument would make the essay describe a font it no longer shows, which is worse than leaving it as history. Its listing below says so.
  • The Android fallback arm is unverified by rendering. Fallback metrics for Noto Serif and Roboto are derived from @capsizecss's published metrics, not measured on an Android device — nobody here has one to measure against.
  • This site renders in standards mode; the Claude Design preview does not. Every kit published there reports document.compatMode: "BackCompat" (quirks) because that host's serve wrapper injects markup ahead of the doctype — not our markup, not fixable there. Where the two differ, trust this one.

Changelog

  • ⭐ 9 Aug 2026 — a decorative amber wash, removed from every kit. The owner caught it live on this site: a facts block sitting in a solid amber-fill panel with a 4px amber border, next to a specimen arguing for Airbnb's actual style — near-white cards, thin grey hairlines, colour spent almost nowhere. It was real and repo-wide, not one kit's mistake: 12 instances across every page here, plus the live site's own .cat-line byline and call-to-action card, which carried the same fill (a milder, borderless form, but shipping). All now render background:var(--paper); border:1px solid var(--rule) — no wash, no accent border. The one deliberate amber fill that stays is the "Checked" stamp — a real verification signal, the one case theme.css's own rules call out as legitimate, and it reads clearly on the new near-white background where it nearly vanished on the old tinted one.
