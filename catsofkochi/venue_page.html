Come for the ceiling, not the coffee. Ask at the desk which rooms are open — it changes.

Ginger

Fort Kochi · Written by Shemeer

It is down the lane past the barber, the one with the blue shutter, and you will walk past the entrance twice before you find it. Everyone does. The doorway is a gap in a wall and there is no sign worth the name.

Inside, the courtyard opens up and the noise of Bazaar Road stops completely. That is the thing worth coming for. The café at the back does a fine enough filter coffee and an ordinary sandwich; nobody has ever come back here for the sandwich.

Ask at the desk before you climb. The upstairs rooms are not always open, and whoever is on will tell you either way rather than let you walk up to a locked door.
