    No gate, no ticket — the nets stand on open waterfront along River Road, there to see anytime. Fishermen work mornings and early evenings; that's their day, not opening hours.

    Pepper House

    Pepper House's site showed 11am–7pm on 7 Aug 2026, but it only ever shows one day — and not whether that's the café or the gallery. They will tell you if you ask.
