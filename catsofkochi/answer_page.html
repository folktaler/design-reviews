Kochi Water Metro: times, fares and which boat to Fort Kochi

The boats run 8am to 7pm. Here is which one goes where, what a pass costs, and when it is worth avoiding.

  • South Chittoor – Cheranelloorvia Eloor

For Fort Kochi you want the High Court terminal. It is the busiest of the six and the only one most visitors ever use.

When it gets crowded

The High Court – Fort Kochi boats fill up at weekends and on holidays, and through December to January and April to May. Mid-morning to mid-afternoon is reliably quiet.

Even full, the boats are air-conditioned and the crossing is short. Staff move people on and off quickly.

Take the last boat back and you will be standing. Everyone leaves the fort at the same time.

Tip from Luna

Tickets and passes

The Kochi One card works on the boats and the land metro, so it is the one to get if you are here more than a day. The Kochi1 app does the same job from your phone and also sells land metro tickets. Cash and card at the counter work if neither suits.

Passes are counted in trips, not days: twelve trips for 180 rupees, fifty for 600, or a hundred and fifty for 1,500 over three months.

Grab a front seat — the part nobody photographs is the fort coming up on you, slowly.
