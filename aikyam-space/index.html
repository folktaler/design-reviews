aikyam space · design review · 9 August 2026

Five pages, proposing one change

aikyam space is a room in Fort Kochi that runs three free community clubs. Its website cannot currently tell a neighbour what is on this Saturday. These five pages propose putting the schedule first, and show what the rest of the site looks like once it is.

⚠️ Known gaps — please do not spend your review on these

All of these are already known and none is fixable by design alone. Anything not on this list is fair game.

1. No photographs

Every image is a grey placeholder box at the right aspect ratio. The live site has real photographs of real sessions; none has been copied in.

2. No email address, no phone number, no street address

None is published anywhere on the live site — measured across all 26 pages. Nothing has been invented to fill the hole. Location is a Maps pin only, which is the owner's decision.

3. WhatsApp is deferred

Deliberately absent. It is likely the channel most neighbours would actually use, so it is worth deciding, but it is out of scope here.

4. /membership/ pricing is unresolved

The live page prices in US dollars — “Free $0”, “Tiny Enterprise $50 /year” — for a Kerala community space. No replacement is proposed; the page is listed for unpublishing pending an owner decision.

5. Nav labels are proposals, not final copy

“Stories”, “Residency” and “About” are placeholders for the owner's own words. “Residency” in particular renames “Co-Impact”, which may carry meaning inside aikyam that an outside reader cannot see.

6. Opening hours are derived, not published

The footer's hours come from the club session times, because the space has never published an opening policy. If the room is open outside 2–6 PM, they are wrong and only the owner can say so.

7. Dark mode is undefined

The live site has a working System / Light / Dark selector in its footer, and dark mode really does render. This system is drawn light-only and has no dark values. That is a decision nobody has taken, not an oversight — see the system specimen.

8. The typeface is loaded from Google, not self-hosted

These mockups link Figtree from fonts.googleapis.com so they render correctly on a review server. The system says the production site must self-host it — the live site already opens five third-party origins before any content arrives.

9. One contradiction found, unresolved

The live monthly guide lists “Stitching Lab, 2 PM–6 PM” on Mondays 10, 17 and 24 August. The live clubs page says the club is closed on Mondays. Two published pages disagree, and these mockups follow the clubs page. Somebody has to say which is right.

⛔ 0. The week grid is frozen at Sunday 9 August 2026

Every date in these mockups is hand-written, so the homepage grid is correct on 9 August and wrong on 10 August. Expected in a drawing — but shipped static it would rot exactly the way the monthly diary rots, which is the defect this redesign exists to fix. The schedule is a recurrence rule; dates are a projection of it that must be computed when the page is read, never typed and never baked. The full constraint, including how it layers so nothing false appears without JavaScript, is in COMPONENTS.md. Listed first because it is the one gap that could undo the whole redesign at implementation time.

10. “Coming up” is empty, and that is the deliverable

There is not one future-dated item anywhere on aikyam.space — /tag/events/ holds only the three monthly guides, /event-status/ renders 28 characters. No event has been invented to fill it. Tink Her Hack 3.0 is a past event and stays in the stories section.

11. “Latest from the clubs” is all Stitching, and that is real

Of the nine days written up in August so far, Stitching is the only club with substantive entries; Science has none and Dance has one. No filler row was added to balance it — the imbalance is a writing question the component makes visible.

12. Two facilitator records disagree

/clubs/ credits Aparna N as a “Tiny Entrepreneur”, but the Tiny Entrepreneurs list on /team/ is Haseena, Nasini Asharaf and Mary Gracy — Aparna N is not on it. One of the two pages is wrong.

How these were checked

Each page was rendered in a real 390 × 844 frame and again at 1280, and measured — not read. Ten assertions run on every page, and each one was verified by injecting its own fault and watching it go red first:

⭐ Line length ≤ 65 median / 75 maxpass — added for v2, and it immediately caught two defects in v1
Text column39 characters a line at 390px, 56 at 1280px

Rendering found four defects that reading the CSS would not have: form controls not inheriting the typeface, an invitation set in caption type, a card whose padding cut its line to 31 characters, and a session title whose tap target was 26px until an overlay took the row. All four are fixed and noted in _kit.css.

⭐ The eleventh assertion is the interesting one, because it was missing. It was added while building v2 and immediately failed on components that had already shipped and already been reviewed as clean: .session__body measured 77 characters a line at 1280px, and the story card excerpt 67 — both 16px text inheriting a column sized for 20px body. A gate that does not exist cannot fail, and "it passed" only ever meant "nothing asked". Fixed across every page by capping 16px blocks at 27rem, the value tokens/spacing.css already carried.

⚠️ And the first fault injected to test the new gate measured exactly 65 against a > 65 threshold, so it passed and proved nothing. A self-test that lands on the boundary is not a self-test. The real one measures 140.
