⭐ 1. Headlines: Claude backfills, the writer takes over

Owner ruled Claude writes the backfill headlines, with three guardrails: nothing in a headline that is not in the body; a twenty-entry human spot-check read consecutively rather than at random; and ⭐ backfill only — going forward, the person who writes the entry writes the headline.

⭐ That last one is the important one and it is not about quality. August 7 carried two things — a stitching session and the cats. Only somebody who was there knows which of them mattered. A generated headline can be accurate and still pick the wrong one of the day's two events.

⚠️ Consecutively, not at random, is the right check — errors in generated headlines cluster by pattern, and a random sample of twenty across six months would show one of each while a consecutive twenty shows the pattern repeating.

⛔ 3. The launch gate — the build fails while any placeholder remains

The placeholder is one known URL, so it is a count. ⭐ The failure is never that people are careless — it is that nothing tells them. If three entries in 150 keep the placeholder, nobody notices by eye and the check notices every time.

⛔ Break it on purpose before trusting it: point one entry at the placeholder, watch the build go red, revert, watch it go green. A gate that has never failed is not known to work — and this project has produced two gates today that could not fail at all.

⚠️ Count the needs-photo tag as well as the image URL. They can disagree — an entry given a real photograph with the tag left on, or tagged with the placeholder already replaced — and a gate that checks one of two fields passes on the state where they diverge.

⛔ 4. The bodies may be rewritten — and one thing inside them may not

Owner, 10 Aug 2026: “the reader is important. not the author.” So the backfill may rewrite bodies for clarity, not only supply headlines. ⭐ The order still stands: headline first, rewrite only where the headline cannot carry it — a headline that supplies a missing referent is less invasive than a rewrite. ⛔ Do not rewrite 150 bodies because you now may.

⛔ Rewriting for clarity is not inventing. “The same group returned” → “The dance group returned” supplies a referent that exists elsewhere in the corpus. Adding a detail that appears nowhere is a different act and is still forbidden. Every fact in a rewritten body must be traceable to its source month.

⛔⛔ And the line the ruling does not reach: a quotation is not the narrator. “The reader over the author” is about the person narrating the day — not about the people quoted inside it. “I never thought I'd be able to dance at this age” is evidence because it is somebody's actual words. Tidied, it becomes a claim the organisation makes about itself. ⭐ A rewrite pass that treats a quoted sentence as prose would destroy the most valuable content on this site while looking like an improvement. Detect quotation before rewriting; leave it exactly as found.

⚠️ One property, recorded so it is not drifted into: with ~150 rewritten bodies the archive becomes a clear record of what happened rather than a record in the words of the people who were there. Both are legitimate and he has chosen the first. ⭐ It applies only to the backfill — going forward the team writes their own — so the two halves of the archive will differ in voice, and someone reading June beside next June should know that is a boundary rather than a drop in quality.

⚠️ 5. What this page cannot answer

Whether the slug is stable. If a headline is edited after publication and the slug follows it, every link already shared in a WhatsApp thread breaks — and those are exactly the 60.8% this page is designed for. ⛔ The slug must be frozen at publish and never track a later headline edit. That is an importer and Ghost behaviour question, not a design one, and it belongs beside the redirect discipline in ASTRO-PLAN.md.
