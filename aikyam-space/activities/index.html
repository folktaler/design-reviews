⭐⭐ The headline is what happened; the date is the quiet line under it. Measured on Airbnb, 10 Aug: in a dated record the person is the headline at 16/20/500 and the date is 14px grey metadata. ⛔ That is the opposite of how a diary is built — and it is why the current guide's 30 date-headings scroll past saying nothing. “August 6” tells a reader nothing; “The bags went on sale” tells them everything.

⭐ Months are a selector, not a filter. Nobody browsing a diary wants to filter it — they want to jump. It is also what keeps a long feed navigable, and the months carry real search traffic: May pulls 282 visitors all-time.

Coming up

  • Onam bags go to Local Sustainable Living

    Planned · Friday 5 September

    The stitching team's five new designs, made from handloom fabric and cut pieces, for the Onam event.

⛔⛔ A planned day is not a card, and that is the same rule that makes a past day one. The rule is “no slot without a guaranteed filler” — an entry with a photograph gets a card because the photo fills the well; an entry without one gets no container at all. ⛔ A card with an empty image well is the defect; a record with no well has nothing to leave empty.

⛔⛔ ONE planned entry, not thirty — and this is the whole trap. The August guide pre-created all 30 days and filled the future ones with the schedule line 28 times. ⭐ Clubs are a RULE. They live on /clubs/ and are said once. An activity is an event. If the answer to “what is on Tuesday” is “the usual clubs”, that is not an activity — it is the schedule, and it already has a page. A future entry has to be worth announcing.

⛔⛔ Two of these five happened and have NO photograph — and one of them cannot ever have one. The 1 August entry is a rain closure: nobody photographs a day the room was shut. The 2 August entry is a discussion that simply was not photographed.

⛔ So “a past entry always has a photograph” is not true of the real data — it is true of three of the five written August days, and the exceptions are not edge cases: a closure is a normal thing to record and a normal thing to have no picture of.

⭐⭐ This does not break the model — it is why the rule was written as “no slot without a guaranteed filler” rather than “past entries are cards”. The photograph does not tell you whether a day is past or planned. It tells you which FORM the entry takes. Both forms sit in one feed, in date order, and the reader notices nothing — because neither form has a hole in it. ⚠️ Had the card been the only form, those two days would each show an empty 4:3 well, and the rain day would look like a bug.

The quiet state — designed first

Nothing written since Tuesday

The clubs still run every afternoon, 2–6 PM. See the week.

⭐ Designed before the good state, as “Coming up” was — but designed as an EXCEPTION, because the habit is measured. June carries 29 written days of 30 and July 23 of 31. ⛔ Quiet stretches are not the norm here, which makes this a smaller and calmer page than it would have been on an assumption.

⛔ It names the gap and hands over something true. “Nothing written since Tuesday” is honest and dated; ⛔ never “no recent activity”, which reads as *the room is dead* when the room is open and full. The clubs line is the fact that stays true on the quietest possible day — and it points at the page that owns it rather than restating the schedule here.

⚠️ Still unanswered and it is not a design question: who writes these, and what happens when that person is away. A homepage promises freshness in a way a diary does not, so if the daily record moves to the front page, this state moves with it.

⚠️ Reviewer notes — not page copy

⛔ 1. The corpus has two shapes and a migration will silently drop one

Measured across all six guides: day headings exist only from May onward. April and March have none — while April carries 42 images, the most photographed month on the site. ⛔ A migration written against June and July would parse cleanly, exit zero, and lose April entirely, because the parser would find no days and conclude there was nothing there. Assert a per-month count.

⛔ 2. The bodies still carry the schedule line, and it must be stripped

Every source day begins 2 PM–6 PM | Stitching Lab · Facilitated by… before it says anything. That prefix is the schedule, repeated 22–28 times in every month. ⭐ It is also the entire reason the page felt “too heavy” — not the photographs, which the owner removed to fix a weight problem the duplication was causing. The importer strips it; the feed shows what happened.

⚠️ 3. The headlines here are mine
