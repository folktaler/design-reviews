Why the emoji go

They render differently on every phone, cannot take a colour, and are read aloud by screen readers under their full names — “handshake, medium-dark skin tone” before “Co-Impact”. And 🍪 next to “Clubs” reads as a cookie banner before it reads as a biscuit. Six words do not need pictures.

Unpublishing changes URLs. Anything with inbound links or search traffic should be redirected, not deleted — and nobody has checked which of these six has either. On the sister project this exact step sent 96,883 impressions to a 404.
