Coming up

Nothing one-off announced yet

The clubs above run every week without an announcement — you never need to check for those. This is where a one-off lands: a performance, a workshop, a visiting group. When something is set, it appears here with its date.

This section is empty on purpose and it is the deliverable. Checked 9 Aug 2026: there is not one future-dated item anywhere on aikyam.space. /tag/events/ — the page the nav labels “Activities” — holds only the three monthly guide posts, newest dated 8 Aug. /event-status/ renders 28 characters. /jobs/ renders 6, and its heading says “Events”. Tink Her Hack 3.0 is a past event and stays in the stories section below; it has not been promoted here.

How this degrades, which is the real design question: a permanently empty “Coming up” must not make the page read abandoned. It does not, because the copy points at the thing that is always true — the weekly clubs need no announcement. The emptiness is a statement about a kind of event, not about the space. ⛔ Do not replace this with a hidden section: hiding it means a reader who wondered has no answer, and the heading is what tells them the question was understood.

All three entries are real, verbatim-ish, and dated, taken from the August 2026 monthly post — the writing already happens daily, it is just buried in a 12,854px scroll. That is why this strip costs no new discipline.

Two things a reviewer should push on. (1) All three rows are the Stitching Lab. That is not a design choice — of the nine days written up so far, Stitching is the only club with substantive entries; Science has none and Dance has one (2 Aug, with Thudippu). ⛔ No filler row was added to balance it. If that imbalance is wrong, it is a writing problem this component makes visible rather than hides. (2) How it degrades: every row carries its own date, so the strip ages in public. Past roughly a fortnight the heading should change from “Latest from the clubs” to “Last written up on 7 August” — the date is promoted to the headline and the word “latest” is dropped, so a quiet fortnight reads as a fact rather than as a broken feed. ⛔ Never pad to three rows.

Who works here

About the residency

The count here is 5, and the live page shows 10. That gap is deliberate. /change-makers-in-residence/ lists ten entries, but they are three different kinds of thing: four external organisations (above), one individual researcher (above), and five of aikyam's own initiatives — aikyam fellows, aikyam jobs, aikyam school, PATTIC and OASIS. ⛔ Listing your own projects among your residents doubles the apparent number of partners. They belong on the Residency or About page, not on a “who works here” strip.

And the two sets really are two sets. “Partner organisation who works from the space” and “club facilitator” overlap in exactly one place — Olimalar Foundation. Mary Gracy and Aparna N are people in the Tiny Entrepreneurs programme, not NGOs, so they stay in the club cards where they belong.

The three clubs

All free. All in Malayalam. Limited seats, with preference for low-income and under-served households from Fort Kochi, Mattancherry and nearby areas.

  • Daily 2–6 PM · except Mondays

    Stitching Club

    Fresh cut pieces collected before they reach landfill, cleaned, arranged and stitched into tote bags. Part of the Tiny Entrepreneurs Network.

    • Fee Free — tools included
    • With Mary Gracy
    • In Malayalam

    Just turn up

  • Daily 2–6 PM · except Mondays

    Science Club

    Children and teachers exploring science by doing — asking questions, experimenting, discovering together. Inspired by MOSAIC.

    • Fee Free
    • With Olimalar Foundation
    • In Malayalam

    Just turn up

  • Saturday & Sunday 2–6 PM

    Dance Club

    Bharatanatyam — movement, rhythm and expression. A space to learn and experience it together.

    • Fee Free
    • With Aparna N
    • In Malayalam

    Just turn up

Teaching something else?

Cooking, carpentry, robotics — if you can teach it, come and see the space. We are looking for people who want to work with us over time, not for one session.
