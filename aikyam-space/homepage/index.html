⭐⭐ “Featured activities” is his phrase, and the design answer is that NOTHING IS CURATED. ⛔ A homepage that needs weekly curation decays the moment somebody is busy — the same failure as the quiet state, which this project has already solved once by refusing to depend on an action. A “featured” slot nobody updates is worse than no slot: it is confidently three weeks old.

⭐ So featuring is DERIVED, not decided: an activity with a photograph leads. Somebody already made that judgement — when they picked up a phone and decided the thing was worth photographing. 69 of 105 days carried one, so the signal exists, it is already being produced, and it costs nobody a weekly decision.

⚠️ The honest limit: recency plus a photograph is not the same as “the best thing we did”. If he wants genuine editorial featuring, that is a person's job every week and the design should say so out loud rather than pretend an algorithm is taste. ⭐ This version cannot go stale; an editorial one can, and someone has to want it enough to keep it fed.

⛔ Two of these five have no photograph and neither shows an empty well. They sit in the same grid at the same width with less in them — no border, no background, nothing to leave blank. ⭐ That is the rule that has survived every ruling today: no slot without a guaranteed filler. ⚠️ 36 of 105 days had no photo and at activity level the proportion will be worse, so this is the common case, not the exception.

⛔⛔ DENSITY WARNING, and it is the thing most likely to break this layout. The extraction found 214 activities, not 105 days — May alone has 75 across 31 days. ⚠️ That is nearer two and a half a day, not one, so a week is roughly 17 entries. This mockup shows five. The layout has to hold at twelve or more, and it must be checked at that count before it is built — a grid that reads well with five and becomes a wall at seventeen is a defect nobody sees in a mockup.

⭐ The photograph is what stops it becoming a wall: with roughly a third carrying one, the grid has natural rhythm rather than uniform blocks. ⚠️ If the photo ratio drops, this layout degrades — and that is a content fact, not a styling one.

⚠️ Reviewer notes — not page copy

⭐ 1. What "ignore other bits" was read as

Emphasis, not deletion. The schedule is one line at the top — “Open today, 2–6 PM. Stitching, Curiosity and Reading.” — and a link. ⛔ It is not removed: a neighbour still comes for it, and /clubs/ is the second-busiest page on the site. ⭐ But it is a sentence, not a section, which is what changed.

⚠️ It still obeys the schedule rule: the no-script base states the recurring pattern in day names; only script upgrades it to “today”. ⛔ Never printed from a static build.

⛔ 2. The lead photograph is 16:9 and the grid is 4:3 — deliberately

A lead at 4:3 on a phone fills most of the screen before a reader has seen a single headline. ⭐ 16:9 keeps the first headline above the fold at 390px while still giving the photograph the space that makes it the lead. ⚠️ Which means the same photograph is cropped two ways — and a third time, at 1.91:1, in a shared link's preview card. Three crops of one image, and only one of them is visible on this site.

⚠️ 3. What this mockup cannot show, and it is the biggest risk

Five entries where the real page carries seventeen a week. ⛔ Check the layout at twelve and at twenty before building it. A grid that reads well at five and becomes a wall at seventeen is a defect invisible in a mockup and obvious on the day it ships.

⚠️ And 214 activities across five months changes the archive too — “month by month” means 75 entries for May, which is a page in its own right, not a list. That is a second layout question this kit does not answer.
