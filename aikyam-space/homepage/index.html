⚠️ Reviewer notes — not page copy

⛔⛔ 1. The grid was rejected on evidence gathered while a bug was live

Sequence: he reported “the cards are all different sizes” → I found height="1200" defeating aspect-ratio, photo cards 1300px against 88px → we concluded a photo-less entry reads as a hole beside its neighbours and went single-column → I fixed the bug.

⛔⛔ Nobody re-tested the grid after the fix. ⭐ Post-fix the spread is 3×, not 15× — and the single column was costing 70% of a 2000px screen. The single column was a fix for a bug, not for a design problem.

⭐⭐ And the thing that makes the grid work now is the excerpt. A photo-less entry has a real paragraph where the photograph would have been, so it fills its cell because it IS full — the same finding that retired the typographic-headline hack. ⛔ No empty image well anywhere.

⭐ 2. The four-entry photo-less run is finally in view

Entries 4–7 — Members followed a product · Fabric slowly turned into bags · Shemeer and Rohit · Magic Bus — are four consecutive real entries with no photograph. ⚠️ This is the case the whole layout argument rested on and it had never been tested. It fills a full row and most of another, and reads as text entries rather than as damage — because each one is complete.

⛔ 3. Intrinsic columns, not breakpoints

repeat(auto-fill, minmax(min(19rem,100%), 1fr)) — one column on a phone, three at desktop, from a single rule. ⛔ Breakpoints create widths nobody tested between, and the one media query in this system is on the photo-less indent of the horizontal card, which this layout does not use.

⚠️ 4. The quotation entry in a grid is an unresolved question

A bordered-nothing sitting in a single column is one thing; sitting in a column of cards it is another. ⭐ It is rendered here as the review form — no container, no border, radius 0 — and it now sits beside cards rather than beneath them. ⚠️ Worth a look specifically: it may read as a missing card rather than as a different kind of thing.
