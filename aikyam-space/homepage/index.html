⚠️ Reviewer notes — not page copy

⛔⛔ 1. Derived featuring is DEAD, and I am not preserving it

The hero-plus-grid version featured an activity by giving it a photograph and the top slot. In a flat listing there is no top slot — “leads” collapses into “is newest”, which is not featuring at all.

⭐ So the idea does not survive in a weaker form; it is gone. The photograph now decides the FORM of an entry, not its position — card where there is one, record where there is not. ⛔ Preserving a distinction the layout can no longer express is exactly what the club label taught, and the honest answer is that the ordering is chronological and nothing is featured.

⚠️ If he wants real featuring, it needs a slot and a person — and that is a weekly job, not a rule.

⭐ The 16:9 lead crop goes with it. It existed because “a 4:3 lead on a phone fills the screen before a reader sees a headline”. No lead, no second crop — and the three-way crop problem shrinks to two: 4:3 on the site, 1.91:1 in the preview card.

⛔⛔ 2. The wall is the RUN, not the count — and I had this wrong

I wrote that “about a third carry a photo, so the grid has natural rhythm”. ⛔ Two things were wrong. The real ratio is 121 of 214 — 57%, not a third. And the ratio was never the point.

⭐⭐ Photographs cluster. They appear on the days somebody ran a children's camp and vanish on the days the space held meetings — so the absence correlates with the day's character, not with a coin flip per entry. In this real slice there is a four-card run with no image at all (Magic Bus, Pranava, then two from 12 May). That is the case the layout must survive, and an average cannot predict it.

⚠️ 3. Four of fourteen are not club sessions, and that is an editorial question

Magic Bus visiting. A researcher's survey. A one-to-one fundraising conversation between two named people. An entry that is nothing but a visitor's quotation. ⭐ The extraction is right to include them — they happened.

⛔ But a homepage “showcasing our activities” puts a two-person meeting beside a children's summer camp, same size, equal weight. ⚠️ Nobody has been asked whether that is wanted. Either everything that happened reaches the front page, or somebody decides — which is the curation problem arriving from the other side.

⛔ 4. An entry that is ONLY a quotation should not be a card

Three entries in the corpus are entirely a direct quotation. “A visitor wrote down how the space felt” is one of them — the body is Rohit Sharma's sentence and nothing else.

⭐⭐ The rule already answers it: a card frames a thing that happened; a quotation is somebody speaking. These want the review form — the same component used for the residents' testimonials on /who-we-are/, measured on Airbnb: no container, no border, radius 0. ⛔ Not built here, because it needs the quote and its attribution together and I would rather show it once, correctly, than sketch it.

⚠️ The last two entries are the repetition problem, shown rather than described. “A craft session for children ran again” and “Another craft session for the children” are consecutive days with near-identical bodies. ⭐ The headlines are honest about it — they say again and another rather than dressing it up — and that honesty is what makes the repetition visible here. ⛔ Twenty-six entries are word-for-word duplicates in nine groups, and six dates are nothing but boilerplate. The owner has ruled they all import. He should see what that looks like before it ships, which is why they are in this mockup.

⚠️ 5. What this still cannot show

Fourteen entries against a real week of roughly seventeen, and 75 in May alone. ⛔ “All activities, month by month” means a 75-entry page, which is a layout question in its own right and is not answered here. It needs its own piece of work.
