Community programmes

Clubs

Three clubs run at aikyam space, all free and all in Malayalam. You do not need to sign up. Come to the space at the time below and say who you are.

Seats are limited, and preference goes to low-income and under-served households from Fort Kochi, Mattancherry and nearby areas.

Stitching, step by step

The stitching club runs in four levels. You start wherever you are and move when you are ready — nobody is timed.

LevelWhat you learn
1 · SaverRepair old or damaged clothes and everyday household items.
2 · MakerUp-cycle fabric leftovers from boutiques into bags and pouches; learn screen printing to add value.
3 · Tiny EntrepreneurEarn ₹2,000–5,000 a month from products you make and sell.
4 · Role ModelGet support to start your own stitching club near where you live.
