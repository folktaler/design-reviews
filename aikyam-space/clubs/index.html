Why this page is the schedule

One page carries the times, and everything else links to it. When a time changes it changes here, once.

⛔⛔ REVERSED 10 Aug 2026, and this is the sharpest thing on the page. This note said the monthly guide was wrong to list “Stitching Lab, 2 PM–6 PM” on Mondays 10, 17 and 24 August, because /clubs/ said the club was closed on Mondays — and the owner confirmed /clubs/ was the right one.

⛔ The rewrite says Stitching runs Monday to Saturday. So the guide was correct all along, and the page we ruled in favour of was the wrong one. The earlier decision resolved a real contradiction by picking the wrong side, and the rewrite reverses it silently — nothing announced that the ruling had been overturned.

⭐ Two lessons, and the second is the one worth keeping. First: nobody will now walk to a shut room on 17 or 24 August, so the urgent correction this note demanded is moot. Second, and larger: when two sources disagree, “which one do we trust?” is a worse question than “why do two exist?” Picking a winner felt like resolution and simply recorded a preference — the contradiction survived, and the preference turned out to be backwards.

⭐ That is the argument for one schedule store, stated properly. Not “two copies disagreed and we fixed the wrong one” but two copies can disagree at all. One store cannot contradict itself, so there is never a side to pick.
