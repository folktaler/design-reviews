# Standfirst

**One sentence under the headline that makes a stranger read 1,900 words. At most one per page.**

## Markup

```html
<p data-standfirst>Sometimes, the people who change lives are simply the ones who
know how to listen.</p>
```

## Rules

- It must be about THIS person. A standfirst identical on every page is decoration in the most valuable space on the site.
- It is ink and tight and narrower than the body — it leads, it does not recede.

## Styling

There is no JavaScript component to import. Style with the system's tokens and
attributes: the `data-*` landmark above is the styling hook AND the contract a
check reads. Never rename it to a class of convenience.
