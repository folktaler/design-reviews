Open Collective

The design system behind a fork of Open Collective, and pages built on it. Published so it can be opened on a real device and reviewed by people and models who are not on the machine running the fork.

The premise changed on 9 August 2026. Four pages here argue for a product that was abandoned.

Collectives were going to be private, so Open Collective could show a stranger nothing and the whole donor experience had to be built as a separate app of ours. That was priced at roughly eleven extra things to build, and the Builder chose public collectives instead. The helper app went with it.

Open Collective now does the giving page, her account, emailed sign-in, her history across organisations, receipts and updates — all verified on the running fork. Razorpay is merged into its own contribution flow. Everything is in rupees. What is left to build is configuration, a few lines in one receipt template, and an export preset.

Nothing has been deleted. Each affected page carries a red block at the top saying what changed and what of it survives. Start with the shaping document, which has been rewritten against the new facts.

Read this before reporting anything.

These are static HTML pages. Nothing is wired up — no data, no clicks that go anywhere (the module picker is the one exception: its checkboxes work, but nothing is saved and reloading resets them), no dark mode, no icons (the product uses Lucide throughout and none are embedded here).

The design tokens are not approximations. Every colour, radius and type size is copied from the platform's own stylesheet, and the Inter font files are the ones it self-hosts. Where a page departs from upstream, it says so on the page.

The giving page Now a configuration spec Built because a private collective serves a stranger nothing. Open Collective's own flow does this job now. The words, the amounts, the UPI-first ordering and the refusal of foreign money all survive as settings and tier copy — four of its decisions reversed outright and are marked. Known gaps in this set

  • Nothing here has been redrawn against the new model. The superseded pages are marked, not rebuilt. Nobody has drawn what a public Indian collective, its rupee contribution flow, its expense with a bill attached, or its update actually look like end to end.
  • The three unresolved questions in the shaping document are unresolved here too — whose Razorpay account receives the money, whether one fiscal host holds many organisations, and whether a donor's name is published beside her amount. Each changes a page.
  • No icons. Every place the product shows a Lucide icon, these pages show a plain shape or nothing.
  • No photographs or avatars. Contributor faces are initials on a grey circle.
  • No dark mode. The platform defines a full dark palette; none of it is exercised here.
  • The hero arc is approximated. The product draws it as an SVG; here it is a radial gradient. It is close, not identical.
  • No error, empty or loading states anywhere.
  • Two things are outstanding whatever is decided: five secrets published in Open Collective's public repository must be replaced before anything faces the internet, and nobody has ever production-built the frontend.

How to change it

Do not edit the copy you are reading. This folder is published output and gets overwritten. The source is ~/opencollective/design-system/ on the machine running the fork; changes are made there and republished.

  • Everything visual lives in tokens.css. Change --primary and every button, link and focus ring moves together.
  • Change --radius and every rounded corner in the system follows, because the medium and small radii are derived from it rather than set separately.
  • There is no build step. Edit a file, reload the page.
