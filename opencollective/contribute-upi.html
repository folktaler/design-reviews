You can stop this any time from your own dashboard. Payments are handled by Razorpay; Aikyam India never sees your card or UPI details.

Why this page looks the way it does

  • UPI is first and card is second. Upstream leads with card because its rails are American. For an Indian contributor that ordering is backwards.
  • The fee is shown but not added. The Collective absorbs it. Showing it keeps the promise of transparency; adding it to the total would make a ₹1,000 contribution cost ₹1,023.60, which nobody expects.
  • Monthly is the default. Recurring contributions are what make a budget plannable. This is a deliberate nudge and worth arguing about.
  • “No fee to you” is a badge, not a footnote. UPI genuinely costs the Collective nothing on most transactions, and that is a real reason to choose it.

Known gaps

  • No tier selection — this shows the custom-amount path only.
  • The final step, where Razorpay’s own payment sheet opens, is not drawn. That interface belongs to Razorpay and we cannot restyle it.
  • No error states: declined payment, expired UPI collect request, or a contributor who closes the tab mid-payment. All three happen and all three need designing.
  • Nothing here is wired up. It is HTML, not the product.
